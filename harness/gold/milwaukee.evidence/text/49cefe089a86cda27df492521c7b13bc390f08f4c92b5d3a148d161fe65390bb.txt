BA-350 Series
Specifi cations:
Approved valves shall be Milwaukee Valve BA-350 Series. Valves shall meet or exceed the requirements
of MSS SP-110. Valves shall be 3-piece, bronze body, full-port design (1/2” - 2 1/2”) 3” standard port
and include fl exible RPTFE seats and adjustable PTFE packing. Valves shall have a blow-out-proof stem.
Valves shall be provided from the factory with Type “K” Male or Female or Type “L” Female end copper-
tube extensions. When required, the valves shall be factory cleaned for oxygen service in conformance
with MVES 60-02, NFPA 99C CSA-41. Each cleaned valve shall include protective caps on the copper-
tube ends, and be sealed in protective bags to prevent contamination prior to installation.
