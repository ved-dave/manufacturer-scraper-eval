SPECIFICATION
Sioux Chief 687 Series water heater access box shall be used where 
accessible, valved connection for water heaters is required. Two-box design 
shall include one box for cold-side MIP outlet with ball valve and flow-through 
bypass behind the valve; and second box for hot-side MIP elbow inlet with 
test cap. Ball valve shall be replaceable, 600 WOG with T-handle and 
quarter-turn operation.
Elbow and tailpiece connections shall comply with ASTM standards.
INSTALLATION
See www.siouxchief.com for full installation instructions.
Install in a location that allows for operation, service, maintenance, etc. 
Make all connections per ASTM standards with compliant materials and 
processes. Test with water and inspect all connections for leaks before use.
