Product Description
Catch basins are designed to increase flow capacity of the system and filter out larger debris. 
Catch Basins have the same integral edge rails as their corresponding system channels and 
include a debris basket. Catch Basins can be positioned anywhere within the run of trench 
drain and can even be used as a stand-alone drainage structure.
