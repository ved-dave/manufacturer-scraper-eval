Knockout Flanges
The TKO™ uses an integrally molded test cap that prevents debris 
from entering the drain line during rough-in and allows for testing of 
the DWV system. The TKO's design was introduced over 25 years 
ago and has been improving ever since. Contractors have made it 
the industry's knockout closet flange of choice. They appreciate not 
only its quality but also the completeness of the line – six different 
connection types, in ABS or PVC, one- piece or with a stainless 
steel swivel ring.
Complete. Innovative. Trusted.
Open Flanges
Open closet flanges are a cost-effective alternative in applications 
where a test cap is not needed. These include installations after 
the system is tested or where no testing is performed. Our 
complete line of open flanges includes five different connection 
types, in ABS or PVC, one-piece or with a corrosion-resistant, 
stainless steel swivel ring.
Offset Flanges
Offset flanges are designed to help contractors solve everyday 
problems like a mispositioned pipe or an inconveniently located 
floor joist. What makes the FullFlush™ and DropKick™ unique is 
their full-flow capacity – which will allow a 3" ball will pass
through unobstructed. The FullFlush has a 3" Sch. 40 Hub 
connection, which will fit into 4" pipe. The DropKick’s 3" spigot 
outlet easily connects to any fitting hub. Both are available in ABS 
or PVC and both are standard with a stainless steel swivel ring.

Push-In Flanges
Sioux Chief’s PushTite™ uses a tough, engineered, elastomer 
gasket to seal against the inside wall of the drain pipe. The clean, 
simple installation makes it perfect for repair or remodeling jobs. 
The PushTite works well with most DWV pipe – ABS, PVC, cast iron, 
and even some copper and lead pipes. It has the largest flow 
diameter of any comparable flange on the market, and has smooth 
inner walls to reduce potential clogs. PushTites are available for 
both 3" and 4" drain pipe.
Specialty Flanges
Sioux Chief offers a wide range of closet flanges for specialty 
installations. From cast iron compression flanges for commercial 
settings, to brass rings for repair/remodel projects in homes with 
lead bends. We also offer urinal flanges for connecting wall-
mounted urinals in commercial bathrooms.
Spacer & Repair Rings
Spacer and repair rings are used to repair or adapt closet flanges 
for specific applications, such as when new or thicker flooring is 
added. The QuickSpacer™ installs under the flange, while the 
Raise-A-Ring™ is typically set on top of the flange, and can be 
stacked if necessary. For repairs of cracked or broken flanges, 
installers can fit the stainless steel Ringer™ over the damaged 
flange for a fast, solid repair without removing the damaged flange.
