Installation Methods for HydroTec Maxi Trench Drain Systems
This bulletin describes some installation methods commonly used with the Sioux Chief HydroTec 'Maxi' system. This 
is not intended to limit the acceptable installation methods to those described below, but can be used as a guideline 
or reference as needed. There are many things to consider when deciding on the best method for a job, including  
site conditions, project time line, and type of installation (new construction vs. retrofit). These factors, along with 
engineering plans and local plumbing/building codes should all be considered when choosing the most appropriate 
installation method. Regardless of the method used to install trench drain, the resulting installation must meet the 
minimum recommended standards for the concrete encasement and expansion/control joints. Installation instructions 
and other technical documents can be found at www.siouxchief.com
Footer Method:
The contractor places a low slump footer (2" to 3" slump). The footer is tall enough so that the channels, when placed on 
the footer, sit higher than the finished floor/top of grade elevation. The contractor will use a rubber mallet to hammer 
the channels down into the low slump footer until the grates are level at 1/8" to 3/16" below finished floor elevation. 
When hammered, the channels sink into the footer and the concrete comes up the sides. Once the layout is level, the 
remaining encasement is poured. 
Block Method:
The contractor sets channels on concrete blocks (one at each joint and extreme ends of the run), high enough off the substrate 
to allow for the correct volume of concrete underneath the assembly. Washers and/or sand can be used to shim 
the channels to meet the correct grate elevation. The contractor will need to ensure the security of the assembly so 
the concrete encasement pour does not float/separate the channels. This is usually best accomplished by putting 
concrete around the joints, up the sides of the channels, and pouring the encasement in 2 lifts. The 1st pour bringing 
concrete up 2” on both sides of the channels; the 2nd pour finishes the concrete encasement to grade and should 
commence once the 1st pour has sufficiently stiffened, but can still be consolidated using a concrete vibrator.
Patty Method:
This is a combination of both the footer and block methods. Use low slump concrete patties, placed at the joints, to 
support the channels instead of using blocks. The contractor will use a rubber mallet to hammer the channels down 
into the patties to level at the correct top-of-grate elevation. Encasement should be completed in 2 pours.
TIP: Have the contractor build an A-frame to hang off the chute of the ready-mix truck. The A-frame should be centered over the trench drain 
so roughly equal amounts of concrete fall to both sides of the assembly, this will prevent concrete from pushing the channels off center line 
compared to the concrete encasement being placed from a single side of the assembly (also helps keep concrete off the grates and out of 
the channels). The concrete placement should be completed at a slow and steady pace. 
Hanging Method:
The contractor supports the channels using baton boards to suspend the channels over the “trench out area of 
installation”. The baton board are fastened to form boards running down both sides of the assembly or fastened to 
existing paving. Properly secured baton boards will keep the channels/assembly from floating while the monolithic 
concrete encasement is poured.
Installation Bracket1 Method:
Using the appropriate length rebar/threaded rod, support the channels from the bottom by using the correctly sized 
bracket at channel joints, including both extreme ends of the trench drain. Determine total required invert (concrete 
thickness plus channel height) and set the installation bracket by tightening the bracket to the rebar using set screws. 
Set two brackets to begin the installation and set the channel on the bracket. Then set one bracket at a time to insure 
proper spacing of the brackets. Channel joints should be centered on each bracket. As each channel is set on the 
brackets, tighten the set screws against the channels from both sides of the assembly. Once the entire run is set and 
secured to the correct top-of-grate elevation, pour a monolithic concrete encasement.
1 Available for Maxi 100/150/200 only
