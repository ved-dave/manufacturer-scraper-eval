CTS STRAP
525-02 and 525-03 SERIES
SPECIFICATION
Sioux Chief SimpleStrap™ CTS tube straps shall be used where necessary 
to secure ½" and ¾" CTS tubing in residential or commercial applications. 
Strap is designed to work with Sioux Chief's perforated 525 series support 
brackets to secure horizontal water supply stub outs when penetrating 
through a wall or to stabilize ½" or ¾" vertical pipe runs within the stud 
cavity. The SimpleStrap™ is compatible with copper, PEX or CPVC. Shall 
be IAPMO certified.
MATERIALS
Standard: High impact polypropylene (gray)
Glueable: ABS (natural)
INSTALLATION
Secure bracket (purchased separately) to vertical wood or metal studs. 
Hold strap at an angle and position small round "tooth" through hole on 
support bracket at desired location. Move strap so that the steel bracket 
is captured behind the small vertical ears located above the pipe hole. 
Lift side of the strap opposite tooth up and over to hook onto the bracket. 
Push CTS tube or stub out through pipe hole. One step glue can be used 
on glueable clamp option on horizontal wall penetrations.
