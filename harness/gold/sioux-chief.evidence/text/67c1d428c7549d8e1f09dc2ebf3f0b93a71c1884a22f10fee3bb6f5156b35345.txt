Installation for Non-Matching HydroTec Maxi Catch Basins & Channels
This bulletin is meant to demonstrate one method of using Sioux Chief HydroTec 'Maxi' catch basins that are wider 
than the channels feeding into it. This is not intended to limit the acceptable installation methods to those described 
below, but can be used as a guideline or reference as needed.
Specific job situations may require the use of a system where the catch basin is wider than the connecting channel. 
The Maxi system can be modified to accommodate such situations. By using an end cap sized to fit the opening of the 
catch basin, and cutting/drilling an opening in the end cap, sized and located according to the connecting channel, a 
channel can be installed to outlet into a wider catch basin. Sealant between the channel and end cap can be used as 
desired, with the understanding that the joint will be surrounded and encased by concrete.
As with all installations, engineering plans and local plumbing/building codes should all be followed and all 
installations must meet the minimum recommended standards for the concrete encasement and expansion/control 
joints. Installation instructions and other technical documents can be found at www.siouxchief.com
