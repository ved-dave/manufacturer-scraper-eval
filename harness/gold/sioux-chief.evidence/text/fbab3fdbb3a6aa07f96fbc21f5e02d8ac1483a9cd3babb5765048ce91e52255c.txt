CPVC Stub Out Elbow
• Do not allow CPVC portion of fitting to be heated.
• Radius bend for better flow and less noise.
• Type L copper tube - Combines elbow and cap in one quick, easy stub out fitting.
• Accepts sweat, push-fitting and compression stop valves.
• Prop 65 WARNING
• The CPVC resin used in this product has been tested, and is monitored on an ongoing basis, to assure its chemical compatibility with FlowGuard Gold®, BlazeMaster® and Corzan® piping systems and products made with TempRite® Technology.
