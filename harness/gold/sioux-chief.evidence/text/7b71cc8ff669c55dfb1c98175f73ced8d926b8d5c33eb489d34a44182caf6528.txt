Installation Guide for Maxi/Top 100 End Caps/Outlets
End Caps and end outlets are available for HydroTec Maxi and Top systems. Depending on the model, the trench drain can be 
connected to a 4", 6" or 8" pipe. The PVC pipe connector is only needed to connect a pipe underneath the channel.
Always follow local plumbing and building codes. Wear gloves and eye protection while cutting/assembling

Diameter

(Part. No. 865-TOOL).

End Outlet Installation
1.	Unlock and remove the grate using the Grate-Locking Tool
(Part No. 865-TOOL).
2.	Place the galvanized steel piece inside the channel so the round 
male side fits inside the channel.
3.	Cut the plastic piece following the pre-cut guides so it fits the size of 
the 4" round male galvanized steel part.
4.	Place the plastic piece against the galvanized steel piece so the 
hooks on top of the plastic piece rest on the edge rails of the 
channel.
5.	Insert the 4" PVC pipe connector into the galvanized steel piece
(Part No. 865-4PC)
6.	Replace the grate and lock in place using Grate-Locking Tool.
