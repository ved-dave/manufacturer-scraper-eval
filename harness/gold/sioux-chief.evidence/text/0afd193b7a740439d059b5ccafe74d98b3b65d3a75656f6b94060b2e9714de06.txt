TRENCH DRAIN HYDRAULIC CALCULATION
DETERMINE THE MAXIMUM LENGTH [L] OF A CHANNEL RUN
Example 1: 
Suppose the catchment depth [E] is 90 ft and the rainfall 
intensity is given as 2"/hr.
The Inflow Volume [qr] is found in the table as
9.3 [gal/inch·h] The result is indicated on the y-axis of 
the diagram below.
A corresponding maximum run length [L] of 76.1 ft can 
be found on the x-axis of the diagram for the
HydroTec Maxi 100.
If the installed length of the Maxi 100 channel exceeded 
76.1 ft, it would be unable to properly drain the inflow of 
water. In that case, a greater effective channel height or 
nominal width should be selected, or more frequent 
outlet locations should be used.
