Guide for Radius Installations
HYDROTEC Trench Drains can be installed in a radius by following the instructions below:
Narrower channels and ½ meter channels allow a 
tighter radius, eliminating unacceptable joint gaps.
Make sure not to exceed a gap width of ¹/4″ for Heel 
Safe requirements and ¹/2″ ADA requirements.
Additional steps may be required to meet end user 
requirements if the resulting gap is too large.  Such 
as mitering the channels.
Joint tape may be required to close the gap 
between channels so concrete doesn’t backfill into 
the channel during the encasement pour.
