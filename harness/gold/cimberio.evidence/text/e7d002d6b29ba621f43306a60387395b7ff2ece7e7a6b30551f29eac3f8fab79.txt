Product Description
The CIM 747 no lead, CimPress balancing valve is a high-accuracy flow balancing and measurement valve designed to EN ISO 9001 standards. It is available in sizes from 1/2" to 2" and is made of corrosion-resistant brass. It can be used for both heating and cooling applications and has a working pressure of up to 232 PSI. It features a compact design with a fixed orifice and test points for accurate measurement, a metal-to-metal thread locking mechanism for precise valve setting, a flip-up cap with an Allen key for locking, a heat- and impact-resistant handle with a 360º re-settable index collar and indicator scale, and an EPDM-lined valve plug for increased control and tight shut-off. It has been tested by BSRIA with water containing high levels of air and dirt and demonstrated excellent tolerance to such conditions. It has features a CimPress connection.
All Cimberio valves qualify for the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act and the Buy American Act.
Based on NSF/ANSI 61-2008 Annex G in compliance with section 116875 of The California Health & Safety Code.
