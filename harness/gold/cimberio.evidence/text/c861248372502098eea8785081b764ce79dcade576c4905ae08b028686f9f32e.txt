Product Description
Adjustable Thermostatic Balancing Valve with Anti-Legionella Disinfection Bypass and CimPress Connections
The Cim 778P balancing valves are designed for temperature balancing in domestic hot water recirculation systems. They have an integrated thermostat that allows for easy adjustment of water temperatures without the need to remove anything from the system. This guarantees desired temperatures at each point of the system and minimizes heat loss. The valves also feature a by-pass mechanism that intervenes in the case of thermal disinfection against Legionella. They are available with ball valves, an inline or swing check valve, and/or strainer, and come with CimPress connections. These valves are a great solution for maintaining desired water temperatures and actively managing the growth of Legionella bacteria in domestic hot water systems.
Based on NSF/ANSI 61-2008 Annex G in compliance with Section 116875 of the California Health & Safety Code.
All Cimberio valves qualify for the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act and the Buy American Act.
