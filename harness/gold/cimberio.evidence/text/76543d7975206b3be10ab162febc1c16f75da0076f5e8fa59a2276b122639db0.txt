TDS-746 · 09-24
FIXED ORIFICE BALANCING VALVE
The Cim 746 is a fixed orifice manual balancing valve forged from corrosion-resistant DZR brass and manufactured in compliance 
with ISO 9001. Ideal for HVAC systems, plumbing installations, sanitary systems, and applications with non-corrosive liquids. It 
offers accurate and reliable performance to within ± 5%, regardless of the valve's setting.
The Cim 746 balancing valve has been tested by BSRIA with water containing high air and dirt 
levels. Results demonstrated that the valve retains a high level of accuracy, and repeatability of 
flow measurements even in unfavorable system conditions.
