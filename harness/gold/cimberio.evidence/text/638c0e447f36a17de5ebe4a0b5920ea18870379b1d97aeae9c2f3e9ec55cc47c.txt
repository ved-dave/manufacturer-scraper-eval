Product Description
VARIABLE ORIFICE FLANGED BALANCING VALVE
Engineered for optimal performance, the Cim 3739 is a
variable orifice manual balancing valve
designed to deliver precision and reliability across various systems. Cast from durable iron and manufactured under strict ISO 9001 compliance, this valve is an ideal solution for
HVAC systems
,
plumbing installations
,
sanitary systems
,
domestic water systems
, and applications involving non-corrosive liquids.
Key Benefits
The Cim 3739 excels under challenging conditions. Rigorous testing by BSRIA using water with high air and dirt levels has proven the valve's exceptional
accuracy
and
repeatability
in flow measurement, even in unfavorable system environments.
Whether for heating, cooling, or water systems, the Cim 3739 balances performance and durability, making it the trusted choice for engineers and installers.
