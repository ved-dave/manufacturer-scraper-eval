Features & Benefits 
•	 Offers zone, terminal bypass and end of line applications
•	 Simple integration into the BMS using the Crane FS Modulating (ACT993M), 
3 Point (ACT993TP) and Failsafe (ACT993FS) actuators
•	 Easy mounting to pipework or terminal units in confined spaces due to the compact 
and lightweight design
•	 Isolation function with Class IV leakage up to 225kPa
•	 PN16 rated product, suitable for building services applications and services with up 
to 30% Glycol
