Features & Benefits
•	WRAS Approved for potable water 
applications -10°C to 80°C for sizes 
DN50 - 400
•	Robust, ductile Iron valve bodies
	 for long service life
•	Stainless steel disc for improved
	 strength and a wide range of
	 applications
•	Suitable for wide temperature
	 range -10°C to 120°C
•	Extended size range for application
	 requirements
•	Fully lugged for secure installation
•	Rated PN25 for higher pressure
	 applications
•	Lever (FM638) and Gearbox
	 (FM639) options available

at 25 Bar without the need of 
downstream backing flanges.

SPECIFICATION:  
Butterfly Valve, as per BS EN 593 / ASTM 609A, Fully Lugged 
as per BS EN 558, Concentric design, EPDM liner, Fusion 
Bonded Epoxy paint, Gear operated. Ductile Iron Body, SS 
disc and shaft, PN25 rated, suitable to mount between EN 
1092-2 PN16 flanges. WRAS Approved for potable water 
applications for sizes DN50 - DN400.  Dead End Service: 
Suitable for Bi-directional end-of-line service at 25 Bar 
without the need of downstream backing flanges.
