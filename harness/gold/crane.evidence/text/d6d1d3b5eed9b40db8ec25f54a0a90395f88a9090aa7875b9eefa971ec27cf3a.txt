Features & Benefits
•	The Double Regulating Valve offers an accuracy of ± 5% on all 
settings for precise flow regulation
•	Y-Pattern globe valves with characterised throttling disc tending 
towards equal percentage performance. 
•	Double regulating feature allows valve opening to be set with an 
Allen key
•	Operation of the valve is by means of the Microset handwheel
•	WRAS approved. 
•	The D921 has sufficient authority to give effective regulation over the 
range of flows covered by matching flow measurement devices/valves
•	The D923 low flow regulating valve has an authority matched 
to the range of ultra-low flows covered by the D902 flow
measurement device
