Flanged Bronze Globe Valve 
Features & Benefits
•	Crane bronze globe valves are highly efficient for throttling because 
seat and disc designs provide flow characteristics with proportionate 
relationships between valve lift and flow rate

AVAILABLE OPTOINS: P150 Locking Device
SPECIFICATION: Valves are manufactured in accordance with BS 5154 PN25 for series B ratings with 
‘long’ face-to-face. Design incorporates disc holder which on sizes 15 to 50 slips on to stem and on sizes 
65 and 80 is retained by a threaded ring. Body seat is integeral of the semi-crown type. Sizes 15 to 50 have 
union bonnet; sizes 65 and 80 have a bolted bonnet.
End flanges conform to BS 4504 Section 3.3 with flat face and are normally supplied drilled.
Note: Users attention is drawn to BS 4504 Section 3.3 Clause 8 regarding types of gaskets and mating 
flanges to be used with metric flanged valves.
This valve is not suitable for use on group 1 gases or unstable fluids, as defined by the Pressure Equipment 
Directive 2014/68/EU.*
