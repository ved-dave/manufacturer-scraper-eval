Enjoy Zero Leakage and Easy Maintenance with Our Reliable Solutions
Stainless Steel Construction and a Safety Locking Handle for Enhanced Operational Safety
The Bray Series 5000/6000 Full-Port, three-piece, Quarter-turn Ball Valve is engineered to deliver all the benefits of three-piece ball valves at an economical price including, a heavy-duty, internal-entry blowout proof stem and adjustable, live load packing that ensures zero leakage, low torque, and extended service life.
Available in both investment cast CF8M (316L) Stainless Steel or WCB Carbon Steel, series 5000/6000 valves include a swing-out center section for easy in-line maintenance. All body castings are marked with a foundry heat number for full traceability, ensuring quality and reliability.
For manual valve operation, series 5000/6000 valves feature stainless steel safety trigger locking handles and travel stops that limit handle movement to 90°. The integrally cast ISO 5211 mounting pad ensures seamless alignment and simplified installation of actuators, gear operators, and other actuation and control accessories.
Available with an industry leading warranty, and a reputation for reliability, Bray Series 5000/6000 series three-piece ball valve are the ideal solution for a wide variety of industrial applications.
