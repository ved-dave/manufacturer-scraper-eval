Pressure Ratings:
Valve shall be rated for bubble-tight shut-off at pressure
rating shown below.
Bi-directional Service
(With downstream flanges and disc in closed
position):
All Series 22 and Series 23 Valves:
• 2”-24” (50mm-600mm) 150 psi (10.3 Bar)
Dead-End Service
(No downstream flanges and disc in closed
position):
All Series 23 Valves:
• 2”-12” (50mm-300mm) 75 psi (5.2 Bar)
• 14”-24” (350mm-600mm) 50 psi (3.4 Bar)
