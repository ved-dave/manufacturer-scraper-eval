APPLICATION:
The purpose of equipping a standard Rite® Series Single
Door Wafer or Flanged Type Swing Check Valve with an
external position indicator, is to provide a visual indication of
the internal disc position.
GENERAL DESCRIPTION:
The model SA4 is a standard Rite® Series Single Door
Swing Check Valve that has been converted, featuring a
positive hexagon drive at the disc/hinge/shaft connection.
The shaft extends through and is supported by the body
at one end, and by a bushing in the body at the other
end. O-rings seal the shaft, seal bushing and the body to
prevent leakage. The indicator mounted on the shaft is
designed to provide disc position indication.
