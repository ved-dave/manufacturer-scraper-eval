Triple Offset Valves
The non-rubbing resilient metal-to-metal
sealing system of the Bray Tri Lok Triple
Offset Valve assures bidirectional zero
leakage shutoff in even the most
demanding applications.
The quarter turn design provides
for low emission stem sealing and
simplifies actuation requirements
for on/off, modulating, and ESD
applications. The valve is inherently
fire-safe and fire-tested. Tri Lok’s uniquely
designed field replaceable seat and seal
ring minimizes downtime and insures a
lower cost of ownership.
