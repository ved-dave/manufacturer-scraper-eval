APPLICATION:
The purpose of equipping a standard Rite® Series Single
Door Wafer or Flanged Type Swing Check Valve with
an external backflush lever is to allow external control of
backflush process. Used in applications such as: pipelines for
chemical washes in the food and beverage industry.
GENERAL DESCRIPTION:
The model SA4A is a standard Rite® Series Single Door
Swing Check Valve that has been converted, featuring a
positive hexagon drive at the disc/hinge/shaft connection.
The shaft extends through and is supported by the body
at one end, by a bushing in the body, and by a ball bearing
designed to reduce friction mounted in a bracket on the
opposing side. O-rings seal the shaft, seal bushing and the
body to prevent leakage. The lever is mounted onto the Figure 02: SA4A Rear View Open.
shaft with a hexagonal connection to manually open or
close the valve.
