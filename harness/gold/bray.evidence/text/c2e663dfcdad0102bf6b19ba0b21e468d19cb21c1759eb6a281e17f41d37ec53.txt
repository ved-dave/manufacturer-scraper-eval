Triple Offset Valves
Triple offset butterfly valves are used in place of gate valves and ball valves in critical isolation
applications. Bray’s Tri Lok valve incorporates design features that most other triple offset valves do
not have that improve performance in abrasive, erosive and corrosive environments, and substantially
extend the overall service life of the valve and the value to the customer.
