RECOMMENDED VALVE INSTALLATION
7700ºº
Tri Lok recommends that the valve should be
installed with the shaft horizontal or at least de-
clined from
vertical. This shaft orientation is beneficial to
reduce the possibility of solid particles within the
media depositing in the bearing areas.
