TECHNICAL DATA
OVERVIEW
The Bray Series 942 Vortex Breaker Knife Gate Valve is
engineered for on/off service and isolation of pulp and paper
recycle and rejects applications. The 942 offers superior
performance in demanding, high-cycle services that require
shearing capabilities such as cyclone separators and recycle
high density cleaners.
Bray Series 942 is an engineered solution featuring a standard
replaceable Vortex Breaker designed to interrupt cyclonic
flow formation which causes premature gate damage.
