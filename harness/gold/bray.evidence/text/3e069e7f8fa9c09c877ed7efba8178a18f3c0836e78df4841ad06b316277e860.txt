SERIES 98
SCOTCH-YOKE ACTUATOR
SYMMETRICAL OR CANTED YOKES
The heart of the Series 98 actuator is the scotch yoke. This
mechanism converts linear motion into rotational motion. The High
piston and/or springs directly couple to a rotating yoke with a
slot that engages the sliding blocks.
This type of actuator has a distinct torque curve, which starts
high, then dips toward the middle of the stroke, and ends with
increasing torque — offering an inherent optimization of torque
requirements associated with many valve applications.

> Torque output curve is balanced.

CANTED YOKE
